Our Story

Where OVEEQ
comes from.

OVEEQ was founded on a straightforward observation: too many Saudi businesses were being underserved by their technology partners. Sold products they didn't need, given advice shaped by vendor incentives, and left without real support when things went wrong.

We built OVEEQ to be the opposite of that. Vendor-independent by design. Every recommendation we make is built around what is right for the client, not what earns us a better margin.

We believe that the businesses building Saudi Arabia's future deserve technology that actually works for them. Infrastructure that performs under real conditions. Partners who pick up the phone. Solutions that are still the right fit two years after they were installed.

That belief is not a marketing line. It is the reason OVEEQ exists.

"Too many businesses are sold what is convenient for the vendor. We exist to change that, one client at a time."
OVEEQ Founding Principle
