Common Questions

Frequently asked
questions.

What does an IT systems integrator in Saudi Arabia actually do?

A systems integrator combines hardware, software, networking and security into one working solution, then supports it. OVEEQ handles discovery, design, deployment and support as one accountable team, so you're not coordinating vendors yourself.

Do you help with NCA cybersecurity compliance?

Yes. We provide cybersecurity consulting, risk assessment and security architecture work aligned with Saudi National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA) requirements, prioritised by actual business impact rather than a generic checklist.

Which regions of Saudi Arabia do you serve?

We are headquartered in Riyadh with presence in the Eastern Province (Dammam), and we serve government, semi-government and corporate clients Kingdom-wide.

Why choose a vendor-independent integrator over a single-brand reseller?

A vendor-independent integrator like OVEEQ isn't incentivised to sell a specific brand. We evaluate 30+ technology brands and recommend whichever fits your budget, workload and growth plan best.
